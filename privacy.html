Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

SwaadSe Tiffin is a home tiffin service operating from 37/1 Om Vihar, Kamla Nagar, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282005. This page explains what information we receive when you contact us, and what we do with it.

This website does not collect your data

This website has no contact forms, no accounts, no login, and no payment pages. There is nothing on this site for you to fill in, so we do not collect anything from you while you read it.

The site does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or tracking pixels. It loads no content from social networks. Your visit is not profiled.

What happens when you contact us

Every button on this site either opens WhatsApp or starts a phone call. When you use one:

  • Your name as it appears on WhatsApp, your phone number, and the messages you send become visible to us in that chat.
  • If you place an order, we will also have your delivery address and your plan preference, because we need both to deliver your tiffin.
  • WhatsApp is operated by Meta, not by us. Your use of WhatsApp is governed by Meta's own privacy policy, which we do not control.

How we use it

We use your details only to prepare and deliver your food, to confirm timings, and to collect payment for your order or monthly plan. We may message you about your own order — for example if delivery will be late.

We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone for marketing. We do not add you to broadcast lists or groups without your agreement.

How long we keep it

We keep order details for as long as you are a customer, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our own accounts. WhatsApp chat history stays on the phones involved until it is deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your address and contact details at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp or calling. If you have an active monthly plan, we will need to keep enough information to finish delivering it.

Contact

For anything about this policy, call or WhatsApp 78955 90063, or write to us at 37/1 Om Vihar, Kamla Nagar, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282005.
