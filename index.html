Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to?
We deliver within 5 km of our kitchen at 37/1 Om Vihar, Kamla Nagar, Agra. That covers Kamla Nagar, Dayalbagh, Balkeshwar, Sikandra and the colonies around them. Send us your locality on WhatsApp and we will confirm straight away.
Can I order only one meal?
Yes. A single meal is ₹80 and there is no minimum order. Most of our monthly customers started with one tiffin to check the taste first.
Do you provide monthly subscriptions?
Yes. One tiffin a day is ₹2400 a month and lunch plus dinner is ₹4600 a month, both covering Monday to Saturday. Payment is made at the start of the cycle.
How do I pay?
Cash on delivery, UPI, Google Pay or Paytm. Monthly plans are paid at the start of the cycle. There is no online payment on this website — everything is arranged directly with us.
Do you offer customized meals?
Yes. We can reduce the spice, cook without onion and garlic, change the number of rotis, or leave out an item you do not eat. Tell us what you need on WhatsApp and we will confirm what is possible.
Is the food prepared fresh every day?
Every tiffin is cooked the same morning it is delivered. Nothing is stored overnight and nothing is reheated from the day before.
