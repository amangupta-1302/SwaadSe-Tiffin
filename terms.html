Orders

  • Orders are placed over WhatsApp or by phone. This website does not take orders or payments.
  • An order is confirmed only when we reply to confirm it. A sent message on its own is not a confirmed order.
  • For same-day delivery, please order before 8:00 AM for the morning slot and before 2:00 PM for the evening slot.
  • All food is pure vegetarian. We do not cook or handle non-vegetarian food.

Delivery

  • We deliver within 5 km of our kitchen in Kamla Nagar, at no extra charge.
  • Delivery slots are 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM – 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. We are closed on Sundays.
  • Timings can slip in heavy rain, on festival days or in traffic. We will message you if your tiffin will be late.
  • Please be reachable at the delivery time. If nobody is available and we cannot reach you, the meal is treated as delivered.

Prices and payment

  • Prices shown on our website are current at the time of publishing and may change. The price we confirm on WhatsApp is the price that applies to your order.
  • We accept cash on delivery, UPI, Google Pay and Paytm.
  • Monthly plans are paid in advance at the start of the cycle.

Monthly plans, pausing and cancellation

  • A monthly cycle covers Monday to Saturday. Sundays are not counted as delivery days.
  • To skip a day, tell us by 8:00 PM the previous evening. Skipped days are added to the end of your cycle.
  • Days missed without notice cannot be credited, because the food has already been cooked.
  • To stop a plan mid-cycle, tell us as early as you can. We will settle the remaining balance against meals already delivered.

Food quality and complaints

Every tiffin is cooked the same morning it is delivered and is meant to be eaten within four hours. Please refrigerate it if you are eating later. If something is wrong with your food, tell us the same day with a photo and we will replace the meal or credit it to your plan.

If you have a food allergy or need onion and garlic left out, tell us before ordering. We cook in a shared home kitchen, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.
