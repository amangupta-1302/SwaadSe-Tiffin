Weekly rotation

The website opens on today's day by itself, so you only need to change this when the rotation actually changes — not every morning.

Prices

Whole rupees, numbers only. Each price changes in two places on the website at once — the amount customers see, and the amount written into the WhatsApp message they send you.

Three places quote prices to Google rather than to visitors, and they are not updated from here: the search-result description, the sharing preview, and the price range in the business listing. After changing a price, ask your developer to update those — or see EDITING-GUIDE section 2.

Phone numbers and address

Changing a number here changes every Call and WhatsApp button on the website at once. Write phone numbers as 10 digits — no spaces, no +91.

Your address and main number are also written into the data Google reads for your business listing, into the map link, and into the Privacy and Terms pages. Those four are not updated from here — see EDITING-GUIDE section 5 after you change them.

Delivery windows

Drives the “Next delivery” tag. Times use the 24-hour clock, so 16 means 4 PM. Only change these if your delivery timings change — and if they do, also update the three places on the website itself (see EDITING-GUIDE section 7).
